1. Controller

Ai Powerhouse Swiss AG (SWISS.Ai), Gotthardstrasse 18, 6300 Zug, Switzerland. Represented by Gregor Zebic, CEO. Contact for all privacy matters: gregor@swiss.ai.

2. What data we process and why

  • Business contact data (name, business email, company, role) that a company publishes on its own website or in public registers, used to send you a one-off business message about a personalised video and page. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in business-to-business contact (Art. 31 FADP; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time.
  • Request form (company, name, email, optional phone, time of consent): used to send you the questionnaire link and to contact you about your request. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and steps at your request.
  • Questionnaire answers (what you type about your company, workflows, tools and wishes): used to prepare your free consulting call and the written report. Legal basis: your consent and steps at your request. Please do not enter personal data of clients or employees, or confidential documents.
  • Booking: if you book a call via the Microsoft Bookings link in our email, Microsoft processes your booking details under its own terms.
  • Anonymous usage counts per page (page views, video plays and progress, button clicks). We set no cookies and use no local storage or advertising trackers. A short-lived technical hash of your IP address is used only for abuse protection (rate limiting) and expires within hours; it is not linked to your answers.
  • Email delivery data (sent, delivered, bounced) from our email provider, to run the mailing and honour opt-outs.

3. Processors and recipients

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements or standard terms:

  • Cloudflare: delivery of this website and the form/analytics backend (Cloudflare Workers and KV storage).
  • Resend: sending and receiving email on swissai.deals.
  • Google (Google Sheets): internal log of requests, questionnaire answers and usage counts.
  • Bunny.net: hosting of the video (EU). Your browser connects to Bunny when you play the video.
  • GitHub: hosting of the static pages of this website.
  • Microsoft: our email and calendar (Microsoft 365) and booking page.

Internal notifications about your requests go to named members of our team. We do not sell your data.

4. Transfers abroad

Some providers above may process data outside Switzerland and the EU/EEA, in particular in the USA. Where this happens we rely on adequacy decisions (including the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention

We keep request, questionnaire and log data for 12 months, unless you ask for earlier deletion or a legal obligation requires longer. Personal links to the questionnaire expire after 30 days. If you opt out, we keep only your email address on a suppression list so that we do not contact you again.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict your data, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To stop receiving our emails, reply "No thanks" or "stop" to any of them. Write to gregor@swiss.ai to exercise your rights. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), access to leads is limited to the SWISS.Ai team, and personal links are signed and time-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if you notice a problem, please tell us.

8. Changes

We may update this notice. The current version is always on this page.
